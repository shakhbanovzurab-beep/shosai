# meetings

Shosai's own video call on LiveKit (fr-10, fr-16). "New meeting" opens a room at once and its link is the invitation: it admits directly, signed in or as a guest, with no account and no app. The call runs itself and ends when everyone has left; Leo takes notes on the server track by track, so each speaker is known without diarization. In the call there are chat, kept with the meeting; reactions and raised hands, which are ephemeral; and a whiteboard whose picture ends up in the notes. Video recording and broadcasts are kept from the draft. The slice owns the cabinet's Meetings section (`/app/meetings`), the room page and the admin's LiveKit screen. Named `rooms` until [ADR 0057](../../../../../docs/adr/0057-notes-and-meetings-slice-names.md).

- Plan: [docs/plan/slices/meetings.md](../../../../../docs/plan/slices/meetings.md). Decisions: MR-D1…MR-D15 in the plan and [ADR 0072](../../../../../docs/adr/0072-meetings-slice.md).
- Public surface: `contracts` (`RoomLinks`, which calendar uses for its events' rooms; `PendingEvents`, which calendar binds; `RoomLink`, `RoomNotFoundError`) and `contracts.events` (`meetings.call.started`, `meetings.call.ended`, `meetings.recording.ready`).
- The HTTP routes, the room's metadata, the participants' attributes and the data-channel topics are specified for the cabinet in [frontend/docs/meetings-api.md](../../../../../frontend/docs/meetings-api.md).

## Domain (step A1)

- **Rooms** (`domain/rooms.py`, MR-D1, MR-D2): open until the owner ends it, an operator closes it, the owner's account goes or it idles for an hour; a closed room never opens again and its first close is kept. An optional title of at most 200 characters. LiveKit names `r-<hex>`, the draft's kept verbatim. The ceiling is the plan's `room_participants` capped by the platform's (50, 4 on staging), never below two; a plan without a value takes the platform's.
- **Invitations** (`domain/invites.py`): 12 URL-safe characters for a new room, the draft's up to 64 kept; a token never expires. `/meet/{token}` is canonical; `/j/` and `/rooms/join/` live forever and lead to `/app/meet/{token}`.
- **Principals** (`domain/principals.py`): a user id or `guest-<12 hex>`; each session's identity is `<principal>__<8 hex>`, so one person on two devices keeps both. Rights and bans follow the principal. A display name is 1–100 characters without control characters or bidirectional overrides.
- **Joining** (`domain/joining.py`, MR-D2): refusals in one order — `not_found` (an unknown token and a hidden owner alike), `room_finished`, `removed`, `room_full`. Everyone in a call has the same SFU rights; the owner's powers are meetings' routes, never `room_admin`. The LiveKit token lives 10 minutes, the room pass 12 hours.
- **Calls** (`domain/calls.py`): folded from webhooks keyed by the SFU room's sid — the earliest event is the start, the most participants seen the peak, the finish ends it once — the same in any order and under replays; `live` → `ending` (the SFU finished) → `ended` (handed over to notes).
- **The sweep's rule** (`domain/idle.py`): every 300 s; a room in the SFU is in use; a call live in the rows but gone from the SFU lost its finish; an hour empty since the last finish, or since opening, closes the room, but never with a pending calendar event or a live broadcast, and nothing closes when the SFU cannot be read.
- **Chat** (`domain/chat.py`, MR-D11): text of 1–2 000 characters, 20 messages per 10 s per principal, `seq` from one per call, the packet on topic `chat` as versioned JSON with the id, the sequence, the sender's identity, the author's name and the time.
- **Recordings** (`domain/recordings.py`, MR-D4): `starting` → `active` → `ending` → `complete` or `failed`, only forward, an end state final; a start refused in order — not the owner, room closed, no live call, not in the plan, storage full, already recording, the platform busy.
- **Broadcasts** (`domain/broadcasts.py`, MR-D7): `scheduled` → `live` → `ended`; the stage (host, co-hosts, ≤ 10 speakers) gets in before the end, the audience (≤ 100) only live and through the waiting room when the host turns it on; viewers publish nothing and are hidden.
- **What clients read** (`domain/wire.py`): the room's metadata `{v, room_id, marks: {notes, recording}}` — without the owner's home — and the participants' `device.*` and `hand` attributes.

## LiveKit (step A2)

- **Settings** (`settings.py`): `LIVEKIT_URL` (the clients' signalling, `wss://` outside development), `LIVEKIT_API_URL` (the server API on the plane's network, without a path prefix), `LIVEKIT_API_KEY` and `LIVEKIT_API_SECRET` (≥ 32 characters) — all four or none. Without them the app starts and every call route answers 503 `video_unavailable`; the production gate refuses a part of them.
- **The port** (`application/video_server.py`): `VideoServer` creates, lists and deletes the SFU's rooms, lists a room's sessions, issues a session's pass for the join's 10 minutes and accepts a webhook only when its JWT is signed with the secret over the body's SHA-256 (401 `webhook_rejected` otherwise).
- **The adapter** (`infrastructure/livekit_server.py`, `livekit_wire.py`): `livekit-api` with explicit values only — it never reads LiveKit's own environment variables — a 5 s bound per call, one attempt, no LiveKit Cloud failover; a transport error, a timeout or a 5xx is 503, a 4xx our bug, `not_found` an ordinary answer (a deleted room, a room with nobody). The webhook's hash is compared in constant time. `shosai_meetings_livekit_requests_total`, `…_request_seconds` and `shosai_meetings_webhook_verifications_total` count the calls and verifications (`infrastructure/livekit_metrics.py`); the readiness probe `livekit` runs when LiveKit is configured.
- **Locally**: the `meetings` profile of `compose.dev.yaml` (LiveKit v1.13.11 and its Redis) and `meetings-recording` (egress v1.15.0), on 127.0.0.1 only and on their own ports, with hard limits ([docs/development.md](../../../../../docs/development.md#video-calls-livekit)). The tests against it read `TEST_LIVEKIT_*`; CI starts the profile.

## Tables (step A3)

- **Tables of each home** (`infrastructure/room_records.py`, `call_records.py`; migration `3b6722d0ad2b`): `rooms` (owner, space, optional title, the LiveKit name and the invitation token, both unique and shaped, open or closed with who closed it and when, notes on or off, the calendar event's reference unique per owner), `room_calls` (one per SFU room instance by its sid; `live` → `ending` → `ended` with the moments that go with each, ceiling, peak, the notes meeting and the board's picture to come), `room_bans` (one per room and principal), `room_chat_messages` (a `seq` unique per call, 1–2 000 characters), `room_recordings` (one active per room by a unique partial index, a failure always with its code) and `room_whiteboards` (one scene per room, at most 20 000 elements, 4 MiB of elements and 8 MiB of images, never parsed). Every child row repeats the room's owner and space.
- **Access**: the company rule and, on top of it, a restrictive rule for the application role — only the owner's transaction sees a row, within the owner's spaces (inv-14). A guest acts through meetings' routes, which open the owner's scope for that room. `meetings_room_by_token(text)` and `meetings_room_by_livekit_name(text)`, functions of the tables' owner, answer one room's id, owner and space for the public join and the webhook. The sweep role reads rooms, calls and recordings.
- **A person's data** (`application/account_data.py`): the rooms opened in Personal, with everything they keep, are exported, copied to another home, compared, purged and erased; a workspace's room stays with the workspace (nfr-16) — its declaration comes with workspaces' `WorkspaceDataSpec`.

## The owner's rooms (step A4a)

- **Billing's ceiling** (additive): a plan's `room_participants` (2–1 000, null for the platform's ceiling) in the catalogue (`tariffs.room_participants`, migration `ba9f302663d7`), the entitlement snapshot and its cache — an entry written before the field reads as null — and the admin's plan form. `MEETINGS_ROOM_PARTICIPANTS_CEILING` (50; 4 on staging) caps it.
- **`/v1/meetings/rooms`** (`channels/http/rooms.py`, `application/rooms.py`, `room_reads.py`): "New meeting" opens a room in Personal or a workspace the person is an active member of, once per `Idempotency-Key`, and answers its link; `GET ?state=live` pages the person's rooms in the space with a call going on, latest call first, with how many are in each from one SFU call (null when the SFU does not answer); the owner reads, renames (empty clears the title), switches notes for and ends a room. Another person's room is `room_not_found`. Switching notes in a live call changes the SFU's marks first — 503 `video_unavailable` changes nothing; ending closes the row for good, then ends the SFU room, and a room the SFU could not end stays closed.

## Joining (step A4b)

- **`POST /v1/meetings/join/{token}`** (`channels/http/joining.py`, `application/joining.py`): ten a minute per address; a signed-in person joins under their profile's name, a guest under the name they give (1–100 characters). The token is looked up in both homes at once through `meetings_room_by_token`; a home that does not answer makes the join 503 only when the other home has no such room. The refusals that need no SFU come first — `not_found` (404, also for an owner in identity's deletion window), `room_finished` (410), `removed` (403) — then the first joiner creates the SFU room with the owner's ceiling (the plan's `room_participants` capped by `MEETINGS_ROOM_PARTICIPANTS_CEILING`, never below two) and the marks, and anyone later is counted against it (`room_full`, 409; egress sessions are not people). Each join is a new session: a LiveKit pass for 10 minutes with the device attributes, and a room pass.
- **Room passes** (`infrastructure/room_passes.py`, `application/passes.py`): opaque random tokens; Valkey keeps their SHA-256 with the room, its home, owner and space, the principal, the session and the author's name, for 12 hours, listed per principal so a removal revokes them all. `POST /v1/meetings/passes/refresh` with `X-Room-Pass` renews one while the room is open and its principal not removed (`room_pass_invalid` 401, `room_finished` 410, `removed` 403); the old pass stops working.

## Removal, hands and the account (step A4c)

- **Removal** (`application/removals.py`): `DELETE /v1/meetings/rooms/{id}/participants/{identity}` bans the session's principal from the room — a person on every device, a guest by their guest principal — revokes their room passes and disconnects every session of theirs; the ban is written first, so a principal the SFU could not disconnect yet is refused everywhere, and a repeat is safe. The owner cannot remove themselves (`cannot_remove_owner`); an identity Shosai did not mint is `participant_not_found`.
- **A hand** (`PATCH …/participants/{identity}/hand` with `{"raised": false}`): the owner clears that session's `hand` attribute.
- **The account** (`application/account_rooms.py`, `channels/subscriptions.py`, `channels/tasks.py`): identity's `account.deletion_scheduled` and `account.blocked` queue `meetings.close_account_rooms`, which finds the person's open rooms with the sweep role, closes each in its space with `account` as its closer — its link then answers `not_found` — and ends the SFU rooms after the commits.

## Webhook, calls and the sweep (step A5)

- **`POST /v1/livekit/webhook`** (`channels/http/webhook.py`): accepted only when signed over its body (401 `webhook_rejected`); `shosai_meetings_webhooks_total{kind}` counts them. A room's events fold into its call at once (`application/calls.py`): the SFU room is found in either home by its LiveKit name; the first event of an instance opens its call once per sid with the instance's ceiling and publishes `meetings.call.started`; every event takes the earliest start and the largest participant count; the finish moves the call to `ending` once, publishes `meetings.call.ended` with its seconds and peak, and queues `meetings.hand_over_call` (`application/handover.py`), which ends the call — the chat, the board and the tracks join it in B2 and B3. Events of other kinds, or of rooms Shosai does not know, are accepted and ignored.
- **The sweep** (`application/sweep.py`, `meetings.sweep_rooms` every 300 s per home): reads the SFU's rooms once and closes nothing when the SFU does not answer; per open room it applies the domain's verdict — in use, a lost finish written as the webhook would, an hour idle since the last call or since opening closed with `idle`, never while a `PendingEvents` binding says its event is to come; closed rooms the SFU still holds are ended there. `shosai_meetings_sweep_rooms_total{verdict}` counts the verdicts.

## Chat (step A6)

- **`/v1/meetings/rooms/{id}/chat`** (`channels/http/chat.py`, `application/chat.py`) with `X-Room-Pass`: a message needs the pass of this room, its room open and its principal not removed, a live call (`no_live_call`), 1–2 000 characters and the sender within 20 messages per 10 seconds per room (GCRA in Valkey; `rate_limited` with its wait). It is kept once per `Idempotency-Key` under the next `seq` — the call's row is locked while the sequence is taken, so concurrent messages are numbered without gaps — and published reliably on topic `chat` after the commit; an SFU that misses it costs nothing, since `GET …?after={seq}` reads what a client missed (200 a page). The handover deletes the call's chat; notes take it from B3 on.

## Video recording (step A7a)

- **Files' `WrittenFiles`** (additive, `files/contracts/written_files.py`): the key of a person's file for an id, the storage left by their plan, and the adoption of an object a writer put there as their File Manager file — at the root, not used by Leo, charged whatever the limit, with `files.file.uploaded`, the same file on a repeat.
- **`/v1/meetings/rooms/{id}/recording`** (`application/recording.py`, `channels/http/recording.py`): the owner starts it on a paid plan (`snapshot.paid`) with storage left, while a call goes on, when the room records nothing and the platform's running egresses are under `MEETINGS_RECORDINGS_CONCURRENT` — in the domain's order. The row is committed `starting` before egress starts a room composite (speaker layout, 960×540, 15 fps, H.264) straight into the owner's home bucket at their file key for the recording's id, with the bucket's credentials in the request (`MEETINGS_EGRESS_S3_ENDPOINT_*` when egress reaches the bucket at another address); the marks say it records. Egress reports from webhooks move the row only forward; the first `complete` (or LiveKit's limit) adopts the file and publishes `meetings.recording.ready`, a failure keeps its code. The sweep stops a recording at `MEETINGS_RECORDING_MAX_MINUTES` and asks the SFU about one whose webhook has not come in two minutes.

## The admin's LiveKit screen (step A8)

- **`/v1/admin/livekit/*`** (`channels/http/admin_livekit.py`) behind the admin session. The overview (`application/admin_watch.py`) reads LiveKit's rooms and egress jobs from its API and its nodes, traffic and day from Prometheus (`application/sfu_metrics.py`, `infrastructure/livekit_load.py`, `MEETINGS_PROMETHEUS_URL`; the `livekit` job of `deploy/observability`). Nothing comes from people's rows, so rooms are LiveKit names, never titles. LiveKit not answering is `down` with no rooms; Prometheus not answering is "no data" — null numbers, no nodes, an empty day; a node it cannot scrape makes the server `degraded`. A node's CPU is counted against the CPUs its Go runtime may use, its memory against `MEETINGS_LIVEKIT_MEMORY_LIMIT_MB`.
- **The admin's actions** (`application/admin_actions.py`): the room is found in either home by its LiveKit name. Ending it closes the row with `admin` as closer and records `room_closed` through identity's `OperatorAudit` in the same transaction, then ends the SFU room; removing a session bans its principal with `admin` as author (`room_bans.banned_by`) and records `room_participant_removed`, then revokes their passes and disconnects them. The owner is not removed (409 `cannot_remove_owner`). An SFU that does not answer leaves the row and the audit and answers 503; a repeat is safe and the sweep ends a closed room.

## Leo's `meetings` toolset (step A13)

- **`channels/agent_specs.py`, `agent_tools.py`, `agent_words.py`** (MR-D8): `meetings.list_live` reads the person's Personal rooms with a call going on — title, invite link, start, count, notes; `meetings.start_call` (compensable) opens a room in Personal as "New meeting" does and returns its invite link and page; `meetings.end_call` (irreversible) closes the planned room for good and then ends its call in the SFU. Both writes wait for the person's card (worded in 16 languages) and are applied once per item key through `ActionItems`; a room the person does not own reads as not found, a closed one as a conflict. The cabinet's section refreshes on the `meetings` domain.

## The external contract (step A16)

- **`tests/features/meetings/test_external_contract.py`** holds the slice's five external routes of the draft's baseline (D1, slice S06) to their contract: LiveKit's webhook is served at `POST /v1/livekit/webhook` and answers as the draft did, 200 with `{"ok": true}`; the four invitation paths `/meet/`, `/j/`, `/rooms/join/` and `/live/` are answered by the edge, whose redirects send each to the room's or the broadcast's page in the cabinet. Meetings has no WebSocket of its own, so there are no frames to hold to the draft's catalogue.
