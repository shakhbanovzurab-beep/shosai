# studio

How Shosai's AI behaves: which models people may choose and what each role runs on by default, the platform's own short layer of instructions, a person's prompts per role and the few words about them ("About you") that every answer to them takes into account.

- Plan: [docs/plan/slices/studio.md](../../../../../docs/plan/slices/studio.md). Decisions: ST-D1…ST-D7 in the plan and [ADR 0074](../../../../../docs/adr/0074-studio-slice.md).
- Public surface (`contracts/`): `MODEL_ROUTES` — the model of a role for a person, within their plan and frontier allowance, with the call's settings and why a choice was replaced; `MODEL_CATALOGUE` — the models a person may choose; `PROMPT_RESOLVER` — the platform layer, the features' addenda, a person's role prompt with its skills and their personal context. Events `studio.model.disabled` and `studio.model.tier_proposed`, both for `alerting`.

## Domain (step 1)

- **Roles** (`domain/roles.py`): `leo` and the five a person sets in Studio — `live_answers`, `screenshots`, `code`, `system_design`, `notes`; no `transcript` (ST-D2). Every model of a role reads text, a screenshot's reads pictures too, Leo's default reads pictures; the personal context goes to every role but `notes`. Without a row a role runs at temperature 0.7, 4 096 output tokens (8 192 for notes), reasoning off.
- **Catalogue** (`domain/catalogue.py`): an OpenRouter model with a slug id of at most 64 characters (a hash ends a long one), its maker and name, window, output limit, what it reads (text out; pictures in → vision) and whether it takes a temperature or reasons — all from OpenRouter's list. The price proposes the tier (frontier above the platform's line); a proposal changes nothing until the admin confirms it, and a declined one is not raised again while the price proposes the same (ST-D4 as amended).
- **Defaults** (`domain/defaults.py`): a role's default and optional fallback are switched on, listed, standard and able to serve the role; a model a default holds cannot be switched off, retired or made frontier.
- **Resolution** (`domain/routing.py`, plan §3.3): the person's choice answers when it is listed, switched on, reads what the role and the call need and is frontier only on a frontier plan; otherwise the default, then the fallback, with the reason (`Substitution`). A standard model falls back to the role's other standard models; a frontier one never across tiers — its `standard` is the default, which the kill switch takes. A frontier choice whose week is used up gives way to the default.
- **Model settings** (`domain/model_settings.py`): a saved model is one the plan offers and that serves the role; the output limit lies in 256…min(the model's, 32 768); reasoning is stored off for a model that cannot reason; a temperature is ignored by a model that takes none.
- **Prompts and context** (`domain/prompts.py`): a name of 1–80 characters, instructions of at most 20 000, each skill once and at most 20; the prompt and its skills fit 12 000 tokens, refused when saved, and a skill that grew later is left out with every skill after it. The personal context is at most 4 000 characters, trimmed.
- **Platform layer** (`domain/platform_layer.py`, `domain/versions.py`, ST-D1): the system prompt, the enabled rules in order and the blocked topics, framed in English; addenda for `meeting_summary` and `voice_mode`. A publish cleans the draft, refuses a draft equal to what is live, checks Latin script by core's `non_latin_letters` — the model-facing test's rule — and the budget (1 000 tokens for the layer, 500 per addendum), and numbers the version; a restore publishes a copy of an old version, checked again.

## The person's Studio (step 8)

- **Routes** (`channels/http/studio_routes.py`, plan §3.5), behind the person's bearer token: `GET`/`PUT /v1/studio/context`; `GET`/`POST /v1/studio/prompts`, `PATCH`/`DELETE …/{id}`, `PUT …/active`; `GET /v1/studio/models`, `PUT …/roles/{role}`. Every row is the person's own: another person's prompt is `prompt_not_found` on every route.
- **Prompts** (`application/person_prompts.py`, `infrastructure/prompt_store.py`): a create runs once per `Idempotency-Key` and counts the person's prompts (≤ 100) under their advisory lock; skills are the person's installed ones, read through agent's `SkillReader` in the same transaction, in order, and the instructions with them fit the role's budget. A prompt moved to another role or deleted leaves the role it served in the same transaction; a role plays only by a prompt of its own role.
- **Context and models** (`application/person_settings.py`): the context stored trimmed; the models the person's plan offers (`MODEL_CATALOGUE`'s rule, frontier only on a frontier plan) and the five roles' settings, an unset role read as its defaults; a role's settings are checked against the plan, the role and the model; an unreadable plan is 503 `entitlements_unavailable`, never a guess.
- **Skills removed** (`channels/subscriptions.py`): on agent's `agent.skill.removed`, consumer `studio.skill_detach` takes the skill off every prompt of the person in one statement; the other skills keep their order.

## Resolution for people (step 10)

- **`MODEL_ROUTES`** (`application/model_routes.py`): the role's settings are one row read in the caller's transaction (`infrastructure/person_reads.py`), the catalogue and defaults the process's snapshot. The choice answers by the domain's rule, else the default with the reason (`shosai_studio_route_substitutions_total` by `studio_role` and `reason` — `role` is the process's label). Billing is asked inside the route: a frontier choice whose week is used up gives way to the default (`frontier_allowance_used`), spent standard tokens raise `TokenLimitError` (402 `token_limit`); a role whose defaults cannot answer raises `ModelRoleUnconfiguredError` (503), counted for its ticket. The person's reasoning switch is `medium` on, `minimal` off for a model that reasons; Leo's model comes as `chosen` from Settings › Leo and its call settings stay agent's. Measured on the local Russian home: one statement, p50 ≈ 3 ms, p95 ≈ 5–7 ms inside the caller's transaction.
- **`PROMPT_RESOLVER`** (`application/prompt_resolver.py`): the platform layer and the addenda from the snapshot; the role's active prompt with its skills' ids in order in one statement, the bodies through agent's `SkillReader` in the same transaction (an uninstalled skill is absent), the skills past the budget left out in order and counted (`shosai_studio_skills_left_out_total`); the personal context one row.
- **Billing** counts a person's background calls of the expensive class against their frontier week (ST-D5), so a frontier choice for notes is paid the same way as anywhere else.
- **Tickets:** `StudioRoleUnconfigured` and `StudioSubstitutionsHigh` (more than 5 % of a role's resolutions in an hour, at least 20), with their runbooks.

## Leo on studio (step 11a)

- Agent resolves a turn's model through `MODEL_ROUTES.route(leo)` at the turn's admission; `ResolvedRoute.token_route` carries billing's route of the call's tokens for the run's record. Settings › Leo's model is checked by `MODEL_CATALOGUE.refusal(person, model, leo)`. Leo's head takes `PROMPT_RESOLVER.platform_layer()` after the policy core and `personal_context()` in segment 4. Until an environment's `leo` role has a default, turns there keep `AGENT_STANDARD_MODEL` — the setting goes with step 11d.

## Leo's studio toolset (step 13)

- `studio.read_context` (read, no provenance: the person's own words) and `studio.update_context` (write: the whole new text, checked and frozen when proposed, shown on a card, applied once per item key through the Action Gateway, compensable, `ToolSpec.mcp = False` — an external agent never rewrites what Leo knows about the person). `channels/agent_tools.py`, `agent_tool_handlers.py`, the card's words in `agent_previews.py` in 16 languages.
