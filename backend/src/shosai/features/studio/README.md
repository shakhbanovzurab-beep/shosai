# studio

How Shosai's AI behaves: which models people may choose and what each role runs on by default, the platform's own short layer of instructions, a person's prompts per role and the few words about them ("About you") that every answer to them takes into account.

- Plan: [docs/plan/slices/studio.md](../../../../../docs/plan/slices/studio.md). Decisions: ST-D1…ST-D7 in the plan and [ADR 0074](../../../../../docs/adr/0074-studio-slice.md).
- Public surface (`contracts/`): `MODEL_ROUTES` — the model of a role for a person, within their plan and frontier allowance, with the call's settings and why a choice was replaced; `MODEL_CATALOGUE` — the models a person may choose; `PROMPT_RESOLVER` — the platform layer, the features' addenda, a person's role prompt with its skills and their personal context. Events `studio.model.disabled` and `studio.model.tier_proposed`, both for `alerting`.

## Domain (step 1)

- **Roles** (`domain/roles.py`): `leo` and the five a person sets in Studio — `live_answers`, `screenshots`, `code`, `system_design`, `notes`; no `transcript` (ST-D2). Every model of a role reads text, a screenshot's reads pictures too, Leo's default reads pictures; the personal context goes to every role but `notes`. Without a row a role runs at temperature 0.7, 4 096 output tokens (8 192 for notes), reasoning off.
- **Catalogue** (`domain/catalogue.py`): an OpenRouter model with a slug id of at most 64 characters (a hash ends a long one), its maker and name, window, output limit, what it reads (text out; pictures in → vision) and whether it takes a temperature or reasons — all from OpenRouter's list. The price proposes the tier (frontier above the platform's line); a proposal changes nothing until the admin confirms it, and a declined one is not raised again while the price proposes the same (ST-D4 as amended).
- **Defaults** (`domain/defaults.py`): a role's default and optional fallback are switched on, listed, standard and able to serve the role; a model a default holds cannot be switched off, retired or made frontier.
- **Resolution** (`domain/routing.py`, plan §3.3): the person's choice answers when it is listed, switched on, reads what the role and the call need and is frontier only on a frontier plan; otherwise the default, then the fallback, with the reason (`Substitution`). A standard model falls back to the role's other standard models; a frontier one never across tiers — its `standard` is the default, which the kill switch takes. A frontier choice whose week is used up gives way to the default.
- **Model settings** (`domain/model_settings.py`): a saved model is one the plan offers and that serves the role; the output limit lies in 256…min(the model's, 32 768); reasoning is stored off for a model that cannot reason; a temperature is ignored by a model that takes none.
- **Prompts and context** (`domain/prompts.py`): a name of 1–80 characters, instructions of at most 20 000, each skill once and at most 20; the prompt and its skills fit 12 000 tokens, refused when saved, and a skill that grew later is left out with every skill after it. The personal context is at most 4 000 characters, trimmed.
- **Platform layer** (`domain/platform_layer.py`, `domain/versions.py`, ST-D1): the system prompt, the enabled rules in order and the blocked topics, framed in English; addenda for `meeting_summary` and `voice_mode`. A publish cleans the draft, refuses a draft equal to what is live, checks Latin script by core's `non_latin_letters` — the model-facing test's rule — and the budget (1 000 tokens for the layer, 500 per addendum), and numbers the version; a restore publishes a copy of an old version, checked again.
