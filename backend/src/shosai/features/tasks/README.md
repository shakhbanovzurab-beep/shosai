# tasks

The one place a person's work is tracked (SM-3): a task written by hand, an action item a meeting produced, a task a colleague assigned in a workspace, a Jira issue on a synced board and a hub's agreed work are the same row, and a change in any view — a Kanban card, Today, a workspace's Tasks screen, a meeting's page, Leo — is the same change everywhere. The slice owns boards with their columns and card order, where tasks sit on a person's boards, the projection of meetings' action items into tasks, workspace tasks under the one access policy, and the two-way Jira sync. The cabinet's feature is `kanban` (SLICE_MAP §4.3).

- Plan: [docs/plan/slices/tasks.md](../../../../../docs/plan/slices/tasks.md). Decisions: TK-D1…TK-D11 in the plan and [ADR 0059](../../../../../docs/adr/0059-tasks-one-store-boards-action-items-and-jira.md); TK-D12, the Kanban golden core, in [ADR 0060](../../../../../docs/adr/0060-kanban-golden-core.md).
- Public surface: `contracts` (`MeetingTasks`, `TaskReader`, `TaskIntake`, `BoardReader`, their types and errors) and `contracts.events` (`tasks.task.assigned`, `.due_changed`, `.completed`, `.reopened`, `.deleted`).

## The API (step 3a)

- `channels/http/tasks.py` — `/v1/tasks`: a list of the tasks the person may read, by due day (no day last) or last change, with its filters and keyset cursors; Today in the person's zone; create once per `Idempotency-Key`; get; patch by field presence; delete and batch delete, all or none. The shapes follow the cabinet's mocks field for field (plan §3.8, §3.11): `channels/http/card_models.py`, `detail_models.py`.
- `application/task_reads.py`, `task_writes.py`, `task_edits.py` — reads under the access policy's predicate; a write finds the task's space, opens its transaction there and locks the row; the owner, and for a workspace's task its admins, delete (TK-D2); each change publishes its events, touches a meeting's set and raises the revision of the reader's board (`scoping.py`).
- `application/recurring.py` — a recurring task marked done makes its next one once, keyed by `recurred_from_id`, at the top of the first to-do column of the completer's board.
- `application/views.py` — names, workspaces, roles, placements and the counts of checklists and comments are read once per list, and only when a task needs them.
- `channels/http/boards.py`, `board_parts.py` (step 4) — `/v1/boards`: the list with the incoming board and the work waiting for it, a board from a template named in the request's language (`domain/template_words.py`), its read, change, layout (`board_changed`), deletion by TK-D6 and the incoming board's choice; columns with colour and collapse, removed with their cards moved to the next column of their kind; cards put, copied and moved, a move setting the status by the column's kind (`application/boards.py`, `columns.py`, `cards.py`). A board read sets the board right first (`application/landing.py`: cards aligned with their statuses, incoming work landed once) and answers in four statements — the board with the incoming choice under its lock, the columns, the cards with their counts and covers as per-row subqueries (`part_reads.counted_columns`), read as rows rather than mapped objects (`task_parts.CardRecord`) — as a Russian home is a 45 ms round trip; Jira's routes answer as without a connection until steps 12–13 (plan §3.14).
- `channels/http/stickers.py` (step 4s) — a person's own stickers with their boards and usage (`/v1/stickers`), changed (states as the full list, a missing state clearing its values) and deleted; stickers placed on a board, shown or hidden — the system ones and the stopwatch's automations on the board's row — and removed (`/v1/boards/{id}/stickers`); a task's values set and cleared (`/v1/tasks/{id}/values/{sticker_id}`): of the person's own sticker or one the task carries, of its kind and bounds (`domain/stickers.py`; `application/stickers.py`, `board_stickers.py`, `values.py`). A board read takes its stickers with its row and a card's values with the card's row as JSON (`application/sticker_views.py`), keeping its four statements; the panel splits the values by the reader's board into `values` and `other_values` (`value_reads.py`) (plan §3.15).
- `channels/http/clocks.py` (step 4a) — a task's stopwatch (`…/stopwatch/start`, `stop`, `reset`: idempotent by state, a span in the task's column on the reader's board, unplaced when it sits on none) and timer (`PUT …/timer` a minute to a year, `pause`, `resume` — `timer_not_set` without one, the overrun below zero — `DELETE`), each answering the three time fields with `server_now` (`application/clocks.py`); plan and fact are `plan_minutes` and `fact_minutes` of `PATCH /v1/tasks/{id}`; a Jira task has none of them. Every card, list item and panel carries `stopwatch`, `timer` and `time_tracking` from the task's row. The board's automations (`application/clock_rules.py`, `domain/clocks.py`) act in the transaction of the change, under the task's lock, only while the board shows the stopwatch: on arrival (a card made there, a recurring task's next one, a task landed as incoming — through `tasks_start_landed`, as the landing runs in the person's own scope), on a move (the old column's span always closes) and on a status changed elsewhere, as the move the changer's next read makes; a read's realignment and other people's boards never act (plan §3.9).
- `channels/http/attachments.py` (step 3c) — a task's attachments (`/attachments`: the person's ready `task_attachment` upload or a File Manager file they may read, held by files on its object, ADR 0064; at most 50, `attachments_full`; a 15-minute download link; a removed attachment clears the cover) and the cover (`PATCH cover_attachment_id`: an image attachment of the task, `cover_not_image`), `application/attachments.py`; previews' links are signed after the reader's transaction for a whole list (`attachment_views.py`); a workspace task takes none until files' step 8b (`storage_not_included`). `application/attachment_releases.py` gives every deleted attachment's held object back to files — after tasks' own deletions and in the tick `tasks.release_attachments` every 15 minutes (`channels/tasks.py`).
- `channels/http/threads.py` (step 3b) — a task's checklist (`/subtasks`: at most 100 lines in fractional order, `subtasks_full`) and its comments (`/comments`: newest first by a keyset cursor, once per `Idempotency-Key`, edited and deleted by the author only), `application/subtasks.py`, `comments.py`; a line or a new or deleted comment raises the revision of the reader's board and touches a meeting's set (plan §3.12).

## Tables (each home, revisions `b3d9e5a7c142`, `c8e4a1f6b293`, `d5f1b8c3e7a4`, `a4c6e8b2d397`, `b7d1f3a9c524`, `c3e5a7b9d168`, `d9f2b4c6e813`)

- `tasks` — the shareable resource `task` (company isolation; `starts_on`, `recurrence`, `leo_fields` and `recurred_from_id` since `c8e4a1f6b293`): a meeting's task also opens to the meeting's readers and its assignee (the access policy's parent and named-reader rules, ADR 0059); the database keeps done with its moment, a member only on a workspace's task, a name only without one, one task per action item.
- `task_subtasks`, `task_comments` — a task's checklist and comments (company isolation, with the task's owner and workspace; deleted with the task): lines ordered by keys unique at commit, comments of 1–5 000 characters by their author.
- `task_attachments` — a task's files, each naming the object files holds for it (one attachment per held object), deleted with the task; `tasks.cover_attachment_id` is an attachment of the same task (a pair key) cleared when it goes; `task_attachment_releases` — the held objects of deleted attachments, named by a trigger in the deleting transaction until tasks gives them back.
- `stickers`, `sticker_states` (company isolation, the owner's: a person's now), `board_stickers` (personal), `task_sticker_values` (company isolation with the task's owner and workspace; exactly one of a state of that very sticker, a number or a text) and `boards.system_stickers`, `boards.stopwatch_automations` (`c3e5a7b9d168`); `tasks_keep_sticker_values` keeps a person's values on workspace tasks as description lines when their stickers go with the account.
- `task_clock_spans` (company isolation with the task's owner and workspace; one running span per task by a partial unique index) and the task's row's sum of them — `stopwatch_since`, `stopwatch_total_ms`, `stopwatch_by_column`, written with the spans under the task's lock so a board reads a thousand running stopwatches without a subquery each — with `timer_length_ms`, `timer_ends_at`, `timer_paused_left_ms`, `plan_minutes`, `fact_minutes`, each checked; `boards.stopwatch_automations` has its shape checked and a cleared `recurrence` is SQL NULL (`d9f2b4c6e813`).
- `meeting_task_sets` — per meeting, the revision of its notes applied and whether a task of it was touched (TK-D3).
- `boards`, `board_columns`, `task_placements`, `task_settings` — personal (user isolation): a card sits in a column of its own board; column positions and card ranks are unique at commit; a column has a colour (one of nine) or none and is collapsed or not (`b7d1f3a9c524`).
- `application/account_data.py` — a person's personal tasks with their checklists, comments and stopwatch spans, sets, boards, columns, placements and settings are exported, copied (placements of personal tasks only) and erased; a workspace's tasks stay with it.

## Locks

A write takes the reader's board before its task (`scoping.lock_boards_of`, ADR 0066): a card's move locks its board, then the task; a change of the task raises that board's revision, so it locks the board first too and the two never wait on each other in a circle. A new write that locks a task goes through `scoping.locked_task`.

## Rules the domain holds

- `domain/tasks.py` — three statuses, an optional priority, where a task came from; a trimmed title of 1–200 characters, a Markdown description up to 10 000; a member does a workspace task, a name as written stands where no member is named; done exactly while `completed_at` is set; Today holds open personal tasks due on or before the day and those completed that day in the person's zone.
- `domain/changes.py` — a change by field presence: an absent field stays, `null` clears; without a due day there is no start, a start is not after the due day, a schedule set without a due day gives the first date from today; a person's change drops Leo's mark from that field.
- `domain/recurrence.py` — every day, every week on chosen days, every month on a day (29–31 on a shorter month's last day); the next due day is the first after the original one, never before today; a range keeps its length.
- `domain/clocks.py` — the stopwatch is the task's: its closed spans in all and per column, a running one since its start; the timer's length from now, paused with what is left and resumed with it, below zero past its end; plan and fact 0–6 000 000 minutes; times in whole milliseconds; a board's automations start on arrival, close the old column's span on a move and then go on in the new one when it ran or the board restarts on a move, unless the task is done and the board stops on done.
- `domain/ranks.py` — a card's place in its column is a fractional key: a move or an insert writes one key between its neighbours; a column whose keys grow past 48 characters is spaced again.
- `domain/boards.py` — ten icons, the `simple` and `project` templates, names and titles of 1–60 characters, 50 boards, 20 columns, 1 000 open cards, every kind of column kept; a layout only reorders and renames what the board shows, and a board that changed meanwhile is `board_changed`.
- `domain/placement.py` — a card shows in a column of its status: one whose status changed goes to the top of the first column of that status; a move to another kind sets the status; a board shows open cards and those done in the last 30 days; what comes to a person lands on their incoming board.
- `domain/action_items.py`, `domain/members.py` — every action item of a kept meeting is a task; a newer revision replaces a meeting's tasks only while none was touched; who does it is the meeting's owner, the name as written, or the one member of that name.
- `domain/jira.py` — status by Jira's category (never `undefined` → done), priority by the default scheme's ids (an unmapped one is never written), due days as calendar days, Atlassian Document Format as plain text.
