# decisions

Leo's Decision Layer: the judgement calls the rest of Leo must not make by hand — which toolsets a turn needs, how carefully a card asks, whether text entering a run tries to instruct the AI, and later what to remember, whether and when to speak first, which mail needs the person — answered as typed outcomes of versioned policies over Jev, a calibrated decision model, behind deterministic rules that only ever make an outcome more careful. The slice has no screen and no route.

- Plan: [docs/plan/slices/decisions.md](../../../../../docs/plan/slices/decisions.md). Decisions: DL-D1…DL-D8 in the plan and [ADR 0069](../../../../../docs/adr/0069-decision-layer-slice.md); the design is the [Leo architecture](../../../../../docs/design/leo/ARCHITECTURE.md) §6 with [ADR 0026](../../../../../docs/adr/0026-leo-decision-layer-on-jev.md).
- Public surface: `contracts` — the port `Decisions` under `DECISION_LAYER` (`decide`, `observe`) and its typed points `TURN_ROUTE`, `ACTION_FRICTION`, `CONTENT_INJECTION`, each with its facts, outcome and observation.

## Policies (step 1)

- A policy (`domain/policy.py`) is one version of a point: its English questions to Jev, the signal each answer gives and the cut it is read through (`domain/verdicts.py`: a threshold with a margin either side where the verdict is unsure), the JSON state Jev reads within its budget (`domain/state.py`: facts computed in code, text in named string fields cut head and tail by their escaped size), the facts a record keeps, and how verdicts become an outcome. `careful(rule, judged)` keeps the final outcome no less careful than the consumer's rule; `settled` says when the rule's answer cannot change, so nothing is asked.
- Thresholds are not in the code: calibration (`domain/calibration.py`) fits each cut for a target precision of one side, with an unsure verdict joining the side its policy reads it as, and measures expected calibration error and accuracy.
- `turn.route@1` (`domain/policies/turn_route.py`): one `noul` per toolset the rule could not fit — is any of its tools needed — and the surely needed ones come first, by probability; never asked while every toolset fits (DL-D3).
- `action.friction@1` (`domain/policies/action_friction.py`): asked only when the rule left a card standard; it warns unless the action surely matches the request and surely stays narrow (DL-D4).
- `content.injection@1` (`domain/policies/content_injection.py`): one `noul` per piece of text under an untrusted label — uploads and attachments included, the person's own words never — and anything but a sure "no" makes the run suspicious (DL-D5).
- Records (`domain/records.py`) keep ids, numbers and codes — the subject, the facts, a keyed hash of the state, the probabilities, the rule's and the final outcome, reason and source — never text and no person's id (DL-D7).
- Every question's wording is registered in the model-facing English test (`policy_wording`).

## Tables (step 2)

- In each home (`infrastructure/records.py`): `agent_decisions` — one row per consulted decision — and `agent_decision_outcomes` — what followed a subject — in monthly partitions kept four months. Neither has a person's id, so no row-level policy: the application may only insert, the sweep role reads them for the daily reliability pass. The database keeps every code closed (point, subject, reason, source, language, outcome) and a state hash at 16 bytes.
- In the EU home: `decision_thresholds` — a version's fitted cuts per revision with its calibration report, numbers only — and `decision_policies` — the version in `shadow`, `active` or `retired` and the revision it uses; unique indexes hold one active and one shadow version per point, and a version in force always names an existing revision. The application reads and adds, and changes a state, never deletes.
- The account data declaration lists both home tables as `expires`: nothing in them outlives its partition, and nothing identifies a person.

## The engine (step 3)

- `DECISION_LAYER` is bound to `application/engine.py`: the point's versions in force (`application/in_force.py`, read from the EU home at most once a minute, the last good copy kept, a stored version whose wording differs from the code's never used); pre-rules — nothing to decide (counted only), a state over budget, no cross-border consent in the Russian home, the process's calls in flight at `DECISIONS_MAX_IN_FLIGHT`, Jev's endpoint not confirmed zero-retention — each the rule's outcome with its reason, recorded; then Jev.
- Jev is asked through the provider port (`application/asking.py`): paid by the person as background work (outside their Leo tokens), effect key `decision:<id>`, the port's deadline three times the policy's (≤ 2 s). The caller waits only the policy's deadline; a later answer lands, is paid once and recorded as `late`. The port hands the call's cost row to the record task (`CostWriter.later`): a decision makes no round trip to the payer's home (7 before, measured; ADR 0069 DL-D2).
- Verdicts, the policy's outcome and the more careful of it and the rule's (`application/judging.py`); a policy that would soften the rule is overruled and counted (`shosai_decision_unsafe_outcomes_total`).
- A shadow version acts on nothing: with the active one's questions it is judged on the same answer under its own cuts, otherwise it asks on its own in the background; alone, the caller gets the rule at once (`application/recording.py`).
- Records and observations go through `infrastructure/record_writer.py`: buffered per home and table, one insert per batch every second or at 500 rows, the rest on stop; a batch a home refuses, or a row past the buffer's cap, is counted lost. On stop the engine first lets its calls in flight land (`drain`).
- The daily task `decisions.check_zero_retention` (EU home) reads OpenRouter's public zero-data-retention list and the pinned model's endpoints (`core/ai/adapters/openrouter_endpoints.py`) and keeps the answer in Valkey for three days (`infrastructure/provider_terms.py`); a status that is not a fresh "yes" makes every point run its rule (`not_zdr`), shown by `shosai_decision_zero_retention`.
- A record's state hash is an HMAC under a key derived from `FERNET_KEY` (`infrastructure/state_key.py`); without the key, records carry none.
- Settings (`settings.py`): `DECISIONS_JEV_MODEL` — the pinned snapshot, never an alias — and `DECISIONS_MAX_IN_FLIGHT`.

## Calibration and the command (step 4a)

- A point's sets live beside the code (`infrastructure/datasets/<point>/`): `synthetic/<language>.jsonl`, `reference/{ru,en}.jsonl` (the person's labels and the machine's beside them) and `recorded.jsonl` — Jev's answers to every case, keyed by the policy's wording, so CI re-reads them without a call and a reworded policy never reuses them (`infrastructure/datasets.py`, `domain/examples.py`). A case's facts are read strictly by the policy (`parse_facts`); a label of the wrong kind is refused.
- The release gates are a pure function (`domain/gates.py`): cuts fitted on the synthetic cases; at least 50 cases on each side of each cut in each of the 16 languages; ECE ≤ 0.05 per cut; accuracy at the policy's floor in every language; a reference of at least 50 cases in Russian and 50 in English, the machine agreeing with the person on 90 %, its accuracy at the floor and within 10 points of the synthetic one, and the careful outcome found in the policy's careful share. `calibrate` writes its run's report beside the run (`report.json`, passing or not), and `tests/features/decisions/test_datasets.py` holds CI to it: a committed set is answered in full under the current wording and its report is the one its run gives — a set never changes without a fresh run. Only `promote` requires the gates to pass (step 5, agent-derived: a set is committed before a person has labelled its reference).
- `shosai decisions` (`composition/decisions_command.py`, role `decisions`): `dataset check`; `calibrate POINT@VERSION` asks every case live as the platform's own call (`application/calibration.py`), writes the recorded run and stores the version's next revision with its report, passing or not — `--recorded` re-reads the last run; `report POINT`; `promote POINT@VERSION --revision N --to shadow|active` refuses a failed or reworded revision and activates only from shadow after two weeks and 5 000 shadow decisions counted in both homes, or a new passing revision of the version already active (`application/promotion.py`); `retire POINT@VERSION` returns the point to its rule within a minute. Revisions and states are kept by `infrastructure/policy_admin.py` in the EU home.

## The generator and the machine's labels (step 4b)

- `shosai decisions dataset generate POINT@VERSION --language L|all --count N` (`composition/decisions_dataset_command.py`, `application/dataset_making.py`): the code chooses each case's labels and the facts it fixes in an even, deterministic rotation (`application/briefs.py`); `DECISIONS_GENERATOR_MODEL` writes the text that makes them true, in batches of eight (four for `content.injection`'s long texts, which overran the answer and lost whole batches to refusals) — four batches at a time, a batch's cases labelled together — in the language named; `DECISIONS_LABELLER_MODEL`, of another provider, answers the policy's own questions about the case blind, and the case is kept only when it agrees. A `content.injection` case that hides an instruction follows one of the agent's injection corpus's patterns (`INJECTION_PATTERNS`: an override with a quiet write, a fake end marker, a fake system message, a data-carrying link or image, full-width markers, another language), written anew in the case's language. Every call is the platform's own; an unreadable answer loses the case, never guesses. New cases number on from the file's highest id, past the ones the labeller dropped.
- `dataset draft-reference POINT@VERSION --language ru|en --count N` writes cases without labels, with the questions to answer, to `reference/drafts/<language>.jsonl` — never filtered by the labeller, whose agreement with the person is what the reference measures: a person labels them blind and moves them into `reference/<language>.jsonl`. `dataset label-reference POINT@VERSION` writes the machine's labels beside the person's on every reference case, never over them (`infrastructure/dataset_files.py`).
- The instructions both models read are English, registered in the model-facing test (`infrastructure/generation_texts.py`).
- The models are cheap by default — `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash` writes, `minimax/minimax-m3.1-flash` labels (≈ $0.0004 a case, twenty times less than the frontier models the first sets used); a labeller that reasons gets 2 000 tokens to finish its answer, and a label of the wrong kind (a `"false"` string) still drops the case.
- `shosai decisions dataset …` stops at the platform's spend ceiling or kill switch: no further call is made, what was kept is written, and the command says to run again once the ceiling resets. A full set (≈ 2 400 cases, ≈ $13) spans more than the `system` account's hourly ceiling of $10.

## `action.friction` beside the agent's rule (step 5a)

- `FRICTION_JUDGE` is bound over the engine (`channels/agent_ports.py`). The agent asks it only for a card its rule left standard (`features/agent/application/friction_judging.py`), with the person's question of the turn and the card as rendered in their language; the decision's subject is the action's id. A card takes a warning only from an active version: a shadow version is asked and recorded, and the card stays as the rule made it; a warning is never asked, so it never becomes standard.
- Each card's end — approved, rejected, expired — reaches `observe` as `CardResolution`, buffered in the person's home, never blocking the decision or the sweep that ended it.
- The set (step 5b): 1 997 synthetic cases in the 16 languages (≥ 53 on each side of each cut in each), written by the generator to the code's labels and confirmed blind — 80 % kept; 60 reference drafts each in Russian and English in `reference/drafts/` for a person to label. The first run (`report.json`): accuracy 0.905–0.966 in every language, above the floor; expected calibration error 0.115 (`matches`) and 0.064 (`wide`) against the 0.05 gate; no reference yet. The point stays on its rule.

## `turn.route` beside the agent's rule (step 6)

- `TURN_ROUTER` is bound over the engine (`channels/agent_ports.py`). The agent asks it only when its rule cannot offer some toolset even for reading (`features/agent/application/route_ranking.py`): while every toolset's reads fit, a write is one `load_toolset` away and nothing is asked (DL-D3). Today a person's catalog is ≈ 7 300 tokens whole and ≈ 3 500 in reads against segment 2's 4 000, so no turn asks.
- When asked, the person's question, the start of the last answer and the thread's turn count are read in one statement; Jev runs while the context is read, and the first model request waits for it at most 400 ms. An active version's ranking — the candidates surely needed, likeliest first — fills the room before the rule's order; a shadow's is only recorded. The decision's subject is the run; what the run called reaches `observe` as `ToolsetsCalled`.
- The set (step 6b): 1 686 synthetic cases in the 16 languages (≥ 85 needed and ≥ 323 not-needed candidates in each), 95 % kept by the blind labeller; 60 reference drafts each in Russian and English. The first run: accuracy 0.73–0.85 per language — under the 0.8 floor in eleven, the measure being the exact set of needed candidates — and expected calibration error 0.16; no reference yet. The point stays on its rule.

## `content.injection` beside the agent's wrapper (step 7a)

- `INJECTION_JUDGE` is bound over the engine (`channels/agent_ports.py`). The agent puts every piece it wraps under an untrusted label to it — tool results and pages, the page's subject, the person's attachments (`file:own`, DL-D5) — the moment the piece is wrapped; the round never waits. The decision's subject is the run and the piece's number; an active version's finding makes the run suspicious, a shadow's is only recorded, an empty piece or an already suspicious run asks nothing.
- The agent awaits the pending judgements, each at most 400 ms, before a write's card and at the run's end: the card says so (`suspicious`, worded in the cabinet's 16 languages) and `agent_runs.taint` keeps the run's final taint for memory.
- The set (step 7b): 1 719 synthetic cases in the 16 languages (≥ 50 on each side in each), seeded by the agent's injection corpus; 119 reference drafts in Russian and English. The first run passes every synthetic gate — expected calibration error 0.024, accuracy 0.93–1.00 — and waits only for the reference.

## Monitors and tickets (step 8a)

- `objectives.py`: the invariant monitor `decision_unsafe_outcome` pages on any outcome less careful than its rule (the engine has already kept the rule's); three tickets on `shosai_decision_requests_total` — decisions refused with `not_zdr` (Jev's endpoint off the zero-retention list or its status unread for three days, 1 %), Jev past the deadline (5 % of asked decisions) and the rule standing in for a fifth of consulted decisions (over three days). The journey «Решения Leo» tracks the time a caller waited, per point; `composition/decisions_dashboard.py` shows who decides, the fallbacks' reasons, late answers and lost records. Runbook: `docs/runbooks/alerts.md#decision-unsafe-outcome` and the SLO section's paragraph on `leo_decisions`.

## Reliability per band (step 8b)

- `decision_reliability` (EU home, revision `c1d09d162c37`): per day and home, the counts of decisions per point, version, revision, shadow flag, band, language and source, and how many the careful outcome's case followed — numbers and codes only. A policy says which band a recorded outcome falls in and what confirms it (`Policy.band`, `Policy.confirms`): a declined card for `action.friction`, a ranked toolset the run called for `turn.route` (bands `ranked` and `none`); `content.injection` observes nothing and counts decisions only.
- The daily task `decisions.count_reliability` (every home, `channels/tasks.py`, `application/reliability.py`) reads the last two full days as the sweep role, grouped in the database by everything counted, and replaces those days' rows in the EU home in one transaction: run twice, or again once a late outcome came in, it leaves the same rows. `shosai decisions report POINT` prints the last week per version, band and source — the rule's and the fallback's rows are the baseline Jev's are compared with.

## `voice.approval` — the owner's bounded exception (step 15)

- The one policy that reads a person's consent (`Policy.reads_consent`): whether the reply to a card read back in a voice conversation clearly approves it (`domain/policies/voice_approval.py`). Its "given" may be less careful than the rule's "not given"; the engine returns it only from an active version's answer in time and counts it (`shosai_decision_consents_total`). Every decision passes `within_bounds`: a changed outcome that leaves any other way is the rule's and pages (`decision_outcome_outside_bounds`).
- `VOICE_APPROVAL_JUDGE` is bound (`channels/agent_ports.py`): approved only for "given" from Jev; without an active version — today — every card is decided on the screen.
