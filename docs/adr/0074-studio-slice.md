# ADR 0074 — The studio slice: a curated catalogue, defaults per role, a short platform layer, five roles a person sets

- **Status:** accepted by the owner on 2026-10-27 ([studio plan](../plan/slices/studio.md) §2): ST-D1, ST-D2, ST-D3, ST-D5 and ST-D6 as written, with the points the plan settles without a decision; ST-D4 with the owner's amendment; ST-D7 on the condition of a measurement, done in the plan's §1.4.
- **Packages:** `studio` (owner); agent (AG-D4 and AG-D11 amended, step 18 closed by studio's step 11, `agent.skill.removed`); notes (step 25 closed by studio's step 12, `notes` role only); billing (a person's background frontier calls counted, BL-D4); decisions (step 14's skill part no longer waits for studio); memory (its models stay its settings); capture (Leo's voice takes the platform layer and the context; live transcription stays the platform's); alerting (`studio.model_disabled`, `studio.model_tier_proposed`); core (`OpenRouterModels`, `OpenRouterCredits.check()`, the 7-day forecast, `ToolSpec.mcp`); M1 (the draft's prompts, contexts and models).
- **Context documents:** [studio plan](../plan/slices/studio.md); [SLICE_MAP](../plan/SLICE_MAP.md) §4.2.19 and SM-5; [ARCHITECTURE.md](../design/ARCHITECTURE.md) §6, §7, §9 (inv-12, inv-13), §10; [Leo architecture](../design/leo/ARCHITECTURE.md) §2.3 (L-N3), §3.3, §7.3, §7.4, §8.5, §13.1; [ADR 0005](0005-data-without-owner-in-eu-home.md); [ADR 0008](0008-error-envelope-with-compatible-detail.md); [ADR 0035](0035-provider-cost-from-reported-usage-and-price-table.md); [ADR 0043](0043-speech-providers-by-task.md); [ADR 0049](0049-one-admin-signed-in-by-env-credentials.md); [ADR 0068](0068-leo-agent-slice.md) AG-D4, AG-D11; [ADR 0071](0071-leo-memory-slice.md) MM-D2; [ADR 0073](0073-recordings-transcribed-with-speakers-by-scribe.md).

## Context

SM-5 gave one slice "how the AI behaves": the model catalogue, defaults and role assignments, the platform's prompts, a person's prompts per role and their personal context. Planning it against the three cabinet screens, the architecture and the draft left questions the documents did not settle:

- **The Platform prompts screen** lets the operator write up to 20 000 characters in any language, with per-request placeholders, in front of every request; choose which agent actions need confirmation; lock people's settings; and set a second default model. Leo §7.3 caps segment 1 and keeps time out of it for the prompt cache; CLAUDE.md requires model-facing text in English; inv-12 makes confirmation the Action Gateway's.
- **The Studio screen** has no Leo role, though AG-D11 put the person's skills into Leo through one; it offers a choice of live transcription model, which inv-13 would turn into unbounded platform spend; it sends the context to "every model request", meeting notes included.
- **Defaults:** one global default on the AI models screen, while a screenshot needs a vision model and notes a text one; today's routes are env settings.
- **The catalogue:** no add and no tier on the screen; OpenRouter lists about 300 models; billing counts tokens by the call's class.
- **Money:** a frontier model chosen for notes would run in the background, which billing does not count.
- **Migration:** the draft kept 32 KiB of context and files of any type; the cabinet has 4 000 characters and no files.
- **Budget:** 4 000 characters of context do not fit segment 4's 600 tokens.

## Decision

1. **A short platform layer (ST-D1).** The Platform prompts screen keeps the system prompt — the platform layer, placed after the code's own policy core — the safety rules and the blocked topics, all English, checked at publish and ≤ 1 000 tokens together; feature prompts for `meeting_summary` and `voice_mode` (≤ 500 tokens each; `email_draft` with `mail`); the draft, numbered immutable versions, publish and restore. The Behaviour tab, `confirm_actions`, `user_overrides` and the placeholders are removed. The layer reaches Leo's text and voice and the desktop answers; notes take only their feature prompt.
2. **Five roles a person sets (ST-D2).** Studio's roles are `live_answers`, `screenshots`, `code`, `system_design` and `notes`. There is no Leo role: Leo's model is chosen in Settings › Leo from studio's catalogue, and Leo's skills come through the agent's own activation (Leo §8.5), not through a Studio prompt — **AG-D11 amended**: `prompt_skills` composes the five roles' prompts only. There is no `transcript` row: live and recorded transcription stay the platform's choice (ADR 0043, ADR 0073). The personal context goes to every answer given to the person — Leo, live answers, screenshots, code, system design — and never to meeting notes.
3. **Defaults per role (ST-D3).** Each of `leo`, `live_answers`, `screenshots`, `code`, `system_design` and `notes` has a default and an optional fallback, set on the AI models screen; a default is enabled, standard tier and capable of its role, and Leo's also reads images. Agent's standard route and notes' model leave the env settings when their consumers switch. Engineering models stay their slices' settings: agent's utility and image models, memory's extraction and embedding, decisions' generator, labeller and Jev, notes' recording transcription, capture's voice and live transcription.
4. **A curated catalogue; tiers the admin confirms (ST-D4, amended by the owner).** The operator adds OpenRouter models by id; their metadata comes from OpenRouter's list at the add and from a daily refresh; prices are F6's; rows are retired, never deleted; a model OpenRouter drops is disabled and reported, a default or fallback only reported. The price proposes the tier — frontier above `STUDIO_FRONTIER_OUTPUT_USD_PER_M` (default $6 per M output tokens) — and **a tier change is never applied silently**: the refresh records the proposal beside the confirmed tier, emits `studio.model_tier_proposed` and reports it in the monitoring chat; the model keeps its confirmed tier for routing, allowances and billing until the admin confirms or declines; a confirmation applies from its moment, and **nothing already counted under the old tier is recounted**. No per-plan defaults.
5. **Background frontier calls are counted (ST-D5).** Billing counts a person's background calls of the expensive class against their frontier week; when it is used up, the role's standard default writes the notes.
6. **No context files (ST-D6).** At M1 a context longer than 4 000 characters keeps its first 4 000, cut at a paragraph or sentence; its full text and each context file become files in the person's Files; nothing of them is injected.
7. **Segment caps (ST-D7, on the owner's condition).** Segment 4: 600 → 3 200 tokens; segment 1: 1 500 → 2 500. Measured through Leo's real turn on the deterministic suite (plan §1.4): the worst first round — the layer at its cap, 4 000 Cyrillic characters and pinned memory at its cap — is 8 740 tokens p50 and 8 820 p95, within L-N3 (≤ 12 K p50, ≤ 20 K p95); a typical context adds 2–4% to a turn's cost on `google/gemini-3.8-flash`, the worst case 43% warm and 70% cold. Agent's step that wires the context measures again on the live suite and on staging and stops for the owner above 12 K p50.
8. **Settled without a decision and accepted:** ADR 0008's codes, worded by the cabinet; platform tables only in the EU home, people's in theirs, no platform rows among people's; a 60-second platform snapshot per process, people's rows read on every resolution; versions as a cursor collection; F6's forecast by the pace of the last 7 full business days; ≤ 100 prompts per person, ≤ 20 skills per prompt, a role's prompt and its skills ≤ 12 000 tokens, `max_tokens` ≤ min(model, 32 768), reasoning off by default; `Idempotency-Key` on creates, publish and restore; `studio.prompts_published` not emitted while nothing consumes it; built-in role prompts and output contracts with the code that parses the output; `agent.skill.removed` detaches skills from prompts.

## Alternatives considered

- **The Platform prompts screen as drawn.** Up to 5 000 tokens in front of every desktop answer, a per-minute placeholder that breaks the cache, and controls — confirmation, locks, a second default — whose effect the code cannot honour or that duplicate Settings › Leo. Rejected.
- **A Leo role in Studio.** Two places would shape Leo's behaviour and its model; skills already have their own activation in Leo's design. Rejected.
- **A person's choice of live transcription model.** Meetings are never stopped by money (inv-13): the platform would pay whatever a person picks. Rejected.
- **Defaults and fallbacks as env settings.** A release to change a product default, and no check against the catalogue. Rejected for product roles; kept for engineering models.
- **The whole OpenRouter list as the catalogue.** Hundreds of rows to tend and a picker no one can read (the draft's 339 models cost 37 K tokens in one tool). Rejected.
- **A tier applied as soon as the price crosses the line** (the plan's first proposal). Rejected by the owner: a person's model would change its counting without anyone deciding.
- **Only standard models for `notes`.** Simpler, but a person's choice would be refused in one role and honoured in the others. Rejected.

## Consequences

- The cabinet's Platform prompts screen loses its Behaviour tab, confirm actions, overrides and placeholders; the AI models screen gains an add dialog, a defaults card and the tier's confirmation; Studio's Models tab has five rows and its context copy says "every answer to you"; Settings › Leo's model row lists the catalogue's text models and Auto.
- Leo's context at the caps grows from ≈ 20 K to ≈ 23.6 K tokens; the growth is paid at the cached rate after the first call of a thread.
- Agent's step 18 is studio's step 11; notes' step 25 is studio's step 12; decisions' tier routing will add a `leo_frontier` default when it ships.
- Until the admin confirms a proposal, a model priced as frontier may count as standard, at the platform's cost; the monitoring chat and a ticket after 3 days keep it visible.
